No. 03If it's your first

First dosa? Three rules.

  1. 01

    Tear from the end.

    Not the middle. The end is where it stays crisp.

  2. 02

    Dip, don't drown.

    Sambar first. Chutney second. Not both at once.

  3. 03

    Eat with your hands.

    We will not judge. Forks are available for the paranoid.

No. 04The kitchen

The recipes came from Guntur. The cooks came with them.

Our head cook grew up in a town two hours south of Hyderabad, where the chillies are grown. He trained in his aunt's dosa cart before she sent him to a Hyderabadi hotel kitchen for the biryani discipline. He has been running our tawa since the day we opened. The ghee podi is his grandmother's recipe. We did not change it.
